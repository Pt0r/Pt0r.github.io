CovenVT

Welcome to the CovenVT portal, thank you for visiting our humble abode. Here you will find general information about our group and our Talents.

CovenVT is a collaborating group of vtubers who decided they would like to help one another in a less formal capacity than a corporation but with more integration than a general group of friends would typically provide.
If you need to contact us outside of the usual community methods please see our about page

CovenVT is settled largely within an expansive forested domain full of mystery and confusion, who needs an explanation when contradicting truths are more convenient. A fusion of worlds cross through the boundaries of our community, visitors cross an ill defined edge, some return home, some stay, some wonder, lost and unsure of what they are doing.
